I am a PhD Candidate in Politics at the Department of Political Economy, King’s College London.

My doctoral project studies why comparable risks are framed differently under conditions of polycrisis and how EU collective securitization strategies are perceived, contested, or accepted by Member States.

More broadly, my research focuses on crisis politics, polycrisis, securitization, comparative politics, blame and responsibility, public opinion and perceptions, and on computational social sciences. My aim is to advance understanding of critical challenges in crisis management and offering novel insights into fostering cohesive and effective collective responses, in a multi-level system of governance.
