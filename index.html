About me

I am a PhD Candidate in Politics in the Department of Political Economy at King's College London. My research investigates how political institutions frame, contest and respond to overlapping crises within the European Union.

My doctoral project focuses on collective securitization under conditions of polycrisis. I examine how the European Commission constructs comparable systemic risks, how national political actors converge with or challenge EU-level narratives, and how the dynamics of multi-level governance shape crisis responses. Energy-related risks are a central empirical focus.

More broadly, I work on crisis politics, comparative politics, public opinion, institutional responsibility and computational approaches to political text. My research combines qualitative analysis with quantitative and computational methods.
