Lectures & Workshops

Guest lecture · April 2026

Polycrisis: Core Concepts and Empirical Analysis

New York University, United Kingdom · Dr Georgios Giannakopoulos' module Europe since 1945

The lecture introduced polycrisis as a concept in political analysis and examined approaches to its empirical study. It considered Europe's experience of entangled crises, particularly since the 2008 financial crisis.

European Law and Governance School, Italy

Co-organised with the ELGS Academic Affairs Team. Designed for students and academic staff interested in polycrisis as both an academic concept and a contemporary policy challenge.

Teaching interests
