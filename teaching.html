My teaching and invited lecture activity focuses on crisis politics, polycrisis, securitization, comparative politics and methodology.

Polycrisis: Core Concepts and Empirical Analysis
New York University, United Kingdom, April 2026
Guest lecture
Description

Guest lecture delivered for Dr Georgios Giannakopoulos’ module 'Europe since 1945'. The lecture introduced the concept of polycrisis, discussed how it can be approached empirically in political analysis and how Europe has been forged under conditions of entangled crises, espacially after the financial crisis of 2008.
